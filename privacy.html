Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mipuka Town ("we", "us", "our") handles information in our mobile games, namely Mipuka Daydream Lane, Mipuka Bandage Bay, Mipuka Chalkboard Club, Mipuka Siren Squad and Mipuka Hose Heroes (together, the "Games"), and on this website, mipukatown.com.

The short version: the Games listed above do not collect, store or share any personal information. They have no accounts and work completely offline.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information from users of our Games. We do not ask for a name, email address, phone number, location, photos, contacts or any other personal data. The Games do not require an account and do not ask you to sign in.

The Games do not use analytics tools, crash-reporting services, advertising networks, tracking technologies or any other third-party software development kit (SDK) that could collect information about you or your device.

2. Data stored on your device

The Games save your play progress (for example the furniture you placed, the clothes you chose and the sound and music settings) only in the local storage of your device, so that the game continues where you left it. This data never leaves your device, is not sent to us or to anyone else, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

3. Internet connection and permissions

Our Games work fully offline and do not need an internet connection to play. They do not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or any other sensitive permission.

4. Children's privacy

We take children's privacy very seriously and we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws. Because the Games do not collect any personal information at all, no personal information about children is ever collected, stored or shared by us. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information in any other way (for example by email), please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

5. In-app purchases

The Games have no in-app purchases. We do not sell anything inside the Games.

6. App stores

When you download a Game from an app store such as the Amazon Appstore or the Apple App Store, that store may collect information under its own privacy policy (for example your purchase history or crash statistics). We do not control that information and we recommend reading the privacy policy of the store you use.

7. This website

This website is a static website. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and it does not ask you for any personal data. If you contact us by email, we use your email address only to reply to your message and we do not share it with anyone.

8. Security

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to lose. Your local game progress is protected by the security of your own device.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when we release a new game. The latest version will always be published on this page with the date of the last update. Continued use of the Games after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

If you have any question about this Privacy Policy or about our Games, please write to us at support@mipukatown.com.
