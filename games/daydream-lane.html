About the game


Welcome to Mipuka Daydream Lane! Mipuka lives with her mom, dad, grandma, grandpa, big brother and baby sibling, and every place on the lane is ready for your stories.

Ride the family bus and choose who comes along, then visit 11 places: the living room, the kitchen, the bathroom, Mipuka's room, the parents' room, the baby room, the garden, the garage, the street, the park and the bakery.

Decorate everything your way: add as many sofas, beds, tables, toys and slides as you like, move them anywhere and pick one of 7 designs for each one. Dress the family in new colours and hair, call more friends and add puppies, kittens and bunnies that hop around the house.

Every place has something to do: open the fridge and cook eggs in the pan, bake a cake in the oven and wash the plates, give a bath with shampoo, a towel and a comb, fix flat tyres and paint cars in the garage, ride the slide, the seesaw and the trampoline, grab an ice cream from the cart, and watch butterflies and birds in the garden.

Features


  • Mipuka and 6 family members who walk, wave, sit, sleep, eat and play
  • 11 places to explore, each with its own activities
  • 45 kinds of furniture and toys, 7 designs each, as many as you like
  • Choose clothes and hair colours for everyone
  • Pets that walk around by themselves
  • Cook, bake, bathe, repair cars and much more
  • No timers, no stages, no losing: play at your own pace
  • Works offline
