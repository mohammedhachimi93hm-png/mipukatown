About the game


Welcome to Mipuka Trolley Treats! The doors of the little supermarket slide open and the whole family has a job: Dad scans the shopping at the checkout, Mom is the store manager, Grandma sells the fruit and vegetables, Grandpa bakes the bread, big brother fills the shelves in the storeroom and Mipuka pushes the trolley.

Ride the green grocery bus, choose who comes along and visit 11 places: the checkout, the deli kitchen, the washroom, the storeroom, the fruit and veg aisle, the kids corner, the trolley park, the loading dock, Main Street, the town park and the bakery corner.

Every place has its own job, done by a character holding the tool: the cashier beeps each item over the scanner, the grocer drops fruit on the scale and the needle swings, the baker slides bread out of the warm oven with a wooden peel, the stacker puts price stickers on the boxes and the ice cream seller scoops a cone. Tap a worker to do the job again, or tap an empty station and someone walks over to help.

Tap the shopping list board for two little shop games: find every picture of the shopping list on the shelf and drop it into the basket, or pay at the till and count the right change back. Every round earns a gold star. There is no timer and no losing.

Make the shop your own: add as many checkouts, scales, trolleys, ovens, basket stacks and ice cream stands as you like, move them anywhere and pick one of 7 colours for each. Ride in the trolley, bounce on the banana ride, fix and wash the delivery vans at the loading dock, cook in the deli kitchen and dress everyone in new colours.

Features


  • A whole supermarket to explore: 11 places, each with its own job
  • Work stations with real tools: scanner, scale, baker's peel, label gun and ice cream scoop
  • Shop games: find the things on the list, and pay and give change at the till
  • Ride in the shopping trolley and on the banana ride
  • Delivery vans to wash, fix and paint at the loading dock
  • Guinea pigs, parrots, tortoises, birds and butterflies
  • Choose clothes and hair colours for everyone
  • No timer and no losing: play at your own pace
