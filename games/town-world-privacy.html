Mipuka Avatar Life Town World Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mipuka Town ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the mobile app Mipuka Avatar Life Town World (the "App").

The short version: we do not collect any personal information. The App is free and shows a small number of ads from Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as child-directed, so no ads are personalised and no one is tracked.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location, and it does not require an account.

2. Advertising

The App shows a banner on its town map and, from time to time, a full-screen ad when a player enters a building. There are no ads while playing inside a place. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

We treat every player as a child. Every ad request is tagged for child-directed treatment and as under the age of consent, and ad content is limited to the "G" rating. With these settings Google does not use the device's advertising identifier, does not show personalised or remarketing ads, and limits the information it collects to what is needed to deliver the ad, count it and prevent fraud, such as the IP address and the device model and operating system.

The App does not ask for permission to track you through App Tracking Transparency, because it never tracks.

Learn more about how Google handles data in child-directed apps: Tag an app for child-directed treatment and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Data stored on your device

The App saves play progress, such as the furniture placed, the clothes chosen and the sound settings, only on your device. This data is never sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

4. Internet connection and permissions

The App can be played offline. It uses the internet connection only to load ads. It does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

5. Children's privacy

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and similar laws. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.

6. App Store

When you download the App from the Apple App Store, Apple may collect information under its own privacy policy. We do not control that information.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the date of the last update.

8. Contact us

If you have any question about this Privacy Policy, write to us at support@mipukatown.com.
