About the game


Welcome to Mipuka Chalkboard Club! The school bell is ringing and Mipuka, her big brother and their classmates are already at their desks. Dad is the teacher today, Mom runs the library, Grandma cooks in the canteen and Grandpa drives the yellow school bus.

Hop on the school bus, pick who rides along and visit 11 places: the classroom, the canteen, the washroom, the art room, the music room, the nap room, the playground, the bus garage, the school street, the sports field and the library.

Tap the chalkboard to play two little school games: count the fruit and pick the right answer for easy sums, or look at an animal and choose its name. Every right answer earns a gold star, and there is no timer and no losing.

Make the school your own: add as many desks, lockers, easels, pianos, drums and playground toys as you like, move them anywhere and pick one of 7 colours for each. Push the roundabout and watch it spin, climb the jungle gym, ride the tyre swing, play the xylophone, paint with brushes and crayons, cook in the canteen, wash the school vans in the garage and dress everyone in new colours.

Features


  • A whole school to explore: 11 places, each with its own activity
  • Chalkboard games: little sums with fruit to count, and animal names
  • Pupils sit at their desks, the teacher sits at the big desk
  • New playground toys: a spinning roundabout, a jungle gym and a tyre swing
  • Desks, lockers, art tables, piano, drums and more, 7 colours each, as many as you like
  • Puppies, kittens, bunnies, birds and butterflies
  • Choose clothes and hair colours for everyone
  • No timers, no losing: play at your own pace
  • Works offline
