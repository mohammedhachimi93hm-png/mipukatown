About the game


Welcome to Mipuka Bandage Bay, the friendliest little hospital by the bay! Dad is the doctor, Mom is the nurse, big brother drives the ambulance, and Grandpa, Grandma and Mipuka are the patients in their cosy pyjamas.

Choose who rides in the ambulance and drive to 11 places: the reception, the kids ward, the doctor's room, the nursery, the cafeteria, the bathroom, the pharmacy, the ambulance garage, the hospital garden, the street and the park.

Patients arrive feeling poorly. Open the medicine cabinet and use the thermometer, the stethoscope, the syrup spoon and colourful plasters until they smile again. Fix flat tyres, clean and paint the ambulances in the garage, cook breakfast in the cafeteria, give a bath with shampoo and a towel, and look after the babies in the nursery.

Decorate every room your way: add as many hospital beds, wheelchairs, desks, cabinets and toys as you like, move them anywhere and choose one of 7 colours for each. Dress everyone in new colours and hair, and add puppies, kittens and bunnies that hop around.

Features


  • A whole family of doctors, nurses and patients who walk, wave, sit, sleep and eat
  • 11 hospital places to explore, each with its own activities
  • Check-ups with a thermometer, stethoscope, syrup and plasters
  • Repair, wash and paint ambulances
  • 50+ kinds of furniture and toys, 7 colours each, as many as you like
  • Pets that walk around by themselves
  • No timers, no stages, no losing: play at your own pace
  • Works offline
