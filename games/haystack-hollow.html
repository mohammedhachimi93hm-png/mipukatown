About the game


Welcome to Mipuka Haystack Hollow! The sun is up on the farm and everyone has a job: Dad milks the cow, Mom brushes the fluffy sheep, Grandma collects the eggs in the hen house, Grandpa waters the vegetables, big brother drives the tractor and Mipuka helps everywhere.

Ride the hay wagon behind the red tractor, choose who comes along and visit 11 places: the big barn, the farm kitchen, the washroom, the cow stable, the hen house, the hay loft, the sheep field, the tractor shed, the farm lane, the veggie garden and the dairy room.

Every place has its own job, done by a character holding the tool: the farmer sits on the stool and milk splashes into the bucket, an egg rolls into the basket, wool puffs float up from the sheep, the watering can makes the vegetables grow, the butter churn shakes and the water pump fills the trough. Tap a worker to do the job again, or tap an empty station and someone walks over to help.

Tap the farm board for two little farm games: find the eggs under the hens and milk the cow until the bottle is full, or pick all the vegetables of one colour from the garden bed. Every round earns a gold star. There is no timer and no losing.

Make the farm your own: add as many cows, sheep, hay bales, fences and vegetable beds as you like, move them anywhere and pick one of 7 colours or coats for each. Drive the big red tractor, fix and wash the little tractors in the shed, cook in the farm kitchen and dress everyone in new colours.

Features


  • A whole farm to explore: 11 places, each with its own job
  • Work stations with real tools: milk bucket, egg basket, brush, watering can, butter paddle and pump bucket
  • Farm games: find the eggs and milk the cow, and pick the vegetables by colour
  • A hay ride behind the tractor and a big farm tractor to drive
  • Little tractors to wash, fix and paint in the shed
  • Piglets, baby goats, ducklings, birds and butterflies
  • No timer and no losing: play at your own pace
