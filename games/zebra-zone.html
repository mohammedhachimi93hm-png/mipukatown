About the game


Welcome to Mipuka Zebra Zone! The zoo gate is open and everyone has a job: Grandpa sells the tickets, Grandma prepares the animal food, Dad is the keeper who brushes the lion, Mom is the zoo vet, big brother is the safari guide who showers the elephant and Mipuka comes to visit in her zebra coat.

Ride the safari bus, choose who comes along and visit 11 places: the ticket gate, the animal kitchen, the washroom, lion land, the zoo clinic, the monkey house, the savanna, the safari garage, the zoo path, the penguin pool and the aquarium cafe.

Every place has its own job, done by a character holding the tool: a ticket pops from the booth, food drops into the bowls, hearts float up from the happy lion, water and bubbles splash on the elephant, the vet listens with the stethoscope and a fish flies to the penguins. Tap a worker to do the job again, or tap an empty station and someone walks over to help.

Tap the zoo map board for two little zoo games: give every animal its favourite food, or wash the muddy elephant with soap and the shower. Every round earns a gold star. There is no timer and no losing.

Make the zoo your own: add as many lions, elephants, giraffes, penguins, palm trees and benches as you like, move them anywhere and pick one of 7 colours for each. Bounce on the zebra ride, fix and wash the safari jeeps in the garage and dress everyone in new colours.

Features


  • A whole zoo to explore: 11 places, each with its own job
  • Work stations with real tools: ticket, food scoop, brush, hose, stethoscope and fish bucket
  • Zoo games: feed every animal, and wash the elephant
  • A zebra spring ride and a safari bus
  • No timer and no losing: play at your own pace
