About the game


Welcome to Mipuka Hose Heroes! The bell rings at the fire station and the whole family is ready: Dad is the firefighter, Mom is the fire chief, Mipuka is a junior firefighter in her red coat, her big brother trains in the yard, Grandma cooks for the crew and Grandpa drives the big red fire engine.

Ride the fire engine bus, pick who comes along and visit 11 places: the station hall, the kitchen, the washroom, the gear room, the bunk room, the quiet room, the training yard, the engine bay, Main Street, the city park and the pizza place.

Tap the safety board to play two firefighter games: tap the little flames in the windows of a house and spray them with the hose until every one is out, or help a kitten waiting at a window by picking the ladder that reaches it. Every rescue earns a gold star. There is no timer and no losing.

Make the station your own: add as many fire poles, hydrants, hose reels, helmet racks, bunk beds and training towers as you like, move them anywhere and pick one of 7 colours for each. Slide down the fire pole, spray water from the hydrant, wash and fix the fire trucks in the engine bay, grab a slice of pizza and dress everyone in new colours.

Features


  • A whole fire station to explore: 11 places, each with its own activity
  • Firefighter games: put out the pretend fires, and pick the ladder that saves the kitten
  • Slide down the fire pole
  • Fire trucks to wash, fix and paint in the engine bay
  • Hydrants, hose reels, helmet racks, bunk beds, a training tower and more, 7 colours each, as many as you like
  • Puppies, kittens, bunnies, birds and butterflies
  • Choose clothes and hair colours for everyone
  • No timers, no losing: play at your own pace
  • Works offline
