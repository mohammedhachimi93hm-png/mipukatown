One Cannon Against Five Worlds

Aim, fire and bring down the tower. Forty stages across five worlds, ten cannons and a new challenge every time.

Coming soon on the App Store

About the game


Every stage gives you a tower of blocks, a limited number of shots and a clock. Match your shot to the blocks, knock out their support and clear the whole tower to win.

Travel through five worlds: Desert, Neon City, Deep Space, Glacier and Ashfall. Each world changes how the game plays, with wind, gravity, ice and chain fire.

Collect ten different cannons, from a riveted naval gun to a plasma orb on a tripod, unlock characters and stands in the shop, and power up your Bomb, Spread and Drill shots.

Features


  • 40 stages across 5 worlds, each with its own rules
  • 10 cannons, each a different machine
  • Bomb, Spread and Drill weapons with 3 upgrade levels
  • A shop with cannons, characters, stands and music
  • Blocks that change every few stages: crates, bottles, tins, fruit, gems and toys
  • Portrait play on iPhone and iPad
