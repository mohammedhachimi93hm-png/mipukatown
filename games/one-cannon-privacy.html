One Cannon Against Five Worlds Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mipuka Town ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the mobile game One Cannon Against Five Worlds (the "Game").

The short version: we do not ask for your name, email or any account. The Game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. The Game does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, photos or contacts, and it does not require an account.

2. Advertising

The Game shows banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, device model, operating system version and how you interact with ads.

In regions where the law requires it, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the Game first shows Google's consent form so you can choose how your data is used for ads.

On iOS, the Game asks for your permission through App Tracking Transparency before the advertising identifier is used for tracking. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalised. You can change this choice at any time in your device settings.

Learn more about how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress, coins, unlocked items and sound settings only on your device. This data is not sent to us and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

4. Internet connection and permissions

The Game uses the internet connection to load ads. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

5. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. App stores

When you download the Game from the Apple App Store, Apple may collect information under its own privacy policy. We do not control that information.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page with the date of the last update.

8. Contact us

If you have any question about this Privacy Policy, write to us at support@mipukatown.com.
