About the game


Welcome to Mipuka Suitcase Skies! The family is off on a trip and everyone has a job at the little airport: Grandma checks in the suitcases and stamps the boarding passes, Mom serves drinks in the plane cabin, big brother waves the batons on the runway next to the big airliner, Grandpa watches the planes through the telescope on the sky deck, Dad is the captain in the cockpit and Mipuka is ready for her holiday.

Fly in the family plane, choose who comes along and visit 11 places: the check-in hall, the sky cafe, the washroom, security, the gate lounge, the plane cabin, the runway, the hangar, the terminal road, the sky deck and the cockpit.

Every place has its own job, done by a character holding the tool: a suitcase drops on the check-in scale and gets its tag, bags ride the belt through the x-ray scanner, the drinks trolley pours juice, the ground crew waves the batons, the wrap machine spins the suitcases and the captain flips the switches in the cockpit. Tap a worker to do the job again, or tap an empty station and someone walks over to help.

Tap the departure board for two little airport games: pack the suitcase with the holiday things (and leave the bathtub at home!), or look at the x-ray screen and guess what is in the bag. Every round earns a gold star. There is no timer and no losing.

Make the airport your own: add as many desks, seats, luggage carts and planes as you like, move them anywhere and pick one of 7 colours for each. Push the luggage cart, fly the spring plane ride, fix and wash the baggage tugs in the hangar, cook in the sky cafe and dress everyone in new colours.

Features


  • A whole airport to explore: 11 places, each with its own job
  • Work stations with real tools: check-in stamp, scanner, drinks jug, batons and the cockpit switches
  • Airport games: pack the suitcase, and guess what is in the bag on the x-ray screen
  • Ride the luggage cart and the plane ride
  • Baggage tugs to wash, fix and paint in the hangar
  • No timer and no losing: play at your own pace
