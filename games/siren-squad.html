About the game


Welcome to Mipuka Siren Squad! The station is open and everyone has a job: Dad is the friendly officer at the front desk, Mom is the chief detective, Mipuka is a young cadet, her big brother helps with traffic, Grandma watches the city map in the control room and Grandpa fixes the police cars.

Ride the police bus, pick who comes along and visit 11 places: the station, the break room, the showers, the detective office, the control room, the rest room, the dog training yard, the car garage, Main Street, the city park and the donut cafe.

Tap the evidence board to play two detective games: look at a dark shadow and find whose shadow it is, or learn the traffic light: red means stop, yellow means wait and green means go. Every right answer earns a gold star. There is no timer and no losing.

Make the station your own: add as many desks, benches, traffic lights, cones, police bikes and dog houses as you like, move them anywhere and pick one of 7 colours for each. Tap the traffic light to change it, light up the city map, wash and fix the police cars, ride the spring motorbike, grab a donut and dress everyone in new colours.

Features


  • A whole police station to explore: 11 places, each with its own activity
  • Detective games: find the shadow, and learn the traffic light
  • An officer at the front desk who hands out walkie-talkies, flashlights, whistles and badges
  • Police cars to wash, fix and paint in the garage
  • Traffic lights, cones, police bikes, a dispatch console and more, 7 colours each, as many as you like
  • Puppies, kittens, bunnies, birds and butterflies
  • Choose clothes and hair colours for everyone
  • No timers, no losing: play at your own pace
  • Works offline
