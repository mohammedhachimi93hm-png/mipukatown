About the game


Welcome to Mipuka Bistro Bites! The little restaurant is open and everyone has a job: Dad is the chef at the big soup pot, Mom makes smoothies at the juice bar, big brother rolls the pizza dough, Grandma decorates the cakes, Grandpa greets the guests at the door and Mipuka is the hungry little guest.

Ride the food truck bus, choose who comes along and visit 11 places: the dining room, the chef's kitchen, the washroom, the pizza corner, the smoothie bar, the kids corner, the sunny terrace, the delivery garage, Bistro Street, the picnic park and the dessert bar.

Every place has its own job, done by a character holding the tool: the host rings the bell and hands out a menu, the chef stirs the soup with the ladle, sauce, cheese and toppings pop onto the pizza, fruit drops into the blender, cream swirls onto the cake and bubbles fly at the sink. Tap a worker to do the job again, or tap an empty station and someone walks over to help.

Tap the menu board for two little restaurant games: make the guest's order by putting the right toppings on the pizza, or set the table with a plate, a cup and a fork for every guest. Every round earns a gold star. There is no timer and no losing.

Make the restaurant your own: add as many tables, chairs, booths, ovens and cake stands as you like, move them anywhere and pick one of 7 colours for each. Ride the delivery scooter, fix and wash the food trucks in the garage and dress everyone in new colours.

Features


  • A whole restaurant to explore: 11 places, each with its own job
  • Work stations with real tools: menu, ladle, rolling pin, piping bag, smoothie cup and sponge
  • Restaurant games: make the order, and set the table for everyone
  • A delivery scooter to ride and a food truck bus
  • Food trucks to wash, fix and paint in the garage
  • Puppies, bunnies, hedgehogs, birds and butterflies
  • Choose clothes and hair colours for everyone
  • No timer and no losing: play at your own pace
